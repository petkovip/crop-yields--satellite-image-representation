## Project context

**Title:** Self-supervised contrastive Sentinel-2 representations for U.S. Corn Belt corn-yield prediction.

**Course:** Yale AMTH 5520 / "Deep Learning: Theory and Application" -- final project, due ~1 week from 2026-04-27.

**Author:** Ivan Petkov (NetID `ipp3`), M.S. Asset Management & Financial Mathematics.

**One-line summary:** Train a domain-tailored SimCLR (`Geo-SimCLR`) on Sentinel-2 satellite tiles with **temporal positive pairs** (same `(fips, grid_idx)` at adjacent biweeks) as the headline novelty; benchmark vs Prithvi-EO-2.0, DINOv2, ConvLSTM (CropNet paper), random init, and proper-NDVI baselines on **county-year corn yield** under **both time and county splits** (per professor feedback).

### Key takeaways

1. **Geo-SimCLR is the strongest single-encoder model on both splits.** Time-split margin over baseline is large (+0.24 R²); county-split margin is narrow (+0.009 R²) because the strong baseline (state one-hot + year + biweekly NDVI LGBM) extracts more state-year structure at 7-state scope than at 3-state.
2. **Frozen Geo-SimCLR + linear Ridge probe BEATS supervised end-to-end ConvLSTM** on time split (+0.669 vs +0.551, margin +0.118 R²). This is a clean SSL win.
3. **DINOv2 is a surprisingly strong RGB-natural-image baseline** despite being trained on natural images (we feed AG bands as RGB slots): +0.661 Ridge time, +0.602 LGBM county. Sits within ~+0.06 R² of Geo-SimCLR on both splits.
4. **Prithvi-EO 300M underperforms** because we channel-pad 3-band AG into 6-band HLS-expected input with 3 zero-padded slots → near-total dimensional collapse (effective rank 1.7/1024 from earlier diagnostic). Documented as Prithvi-as-baseline limitation, not a result against Prithvi itself.
5. **Random ResNet-18 (no SSL)** is a strong control: +0.558 Ridge time, +0.583 LGBM county. Random conv features after BN are not nothing — they capture color/texture statistics correlated with vegetation. Geo-SimCLR's improvement (+0.111 Ridge time, +0.077 LGBM county) is the *clean* SSL gain.
6. **Probe choice depends on split**: Ridge dominates time-split (year-extrapolation + low-dim features favor regularized linear); LGBM dominates county-split (year is in-distribution + tree models capture state-year interactions). Headline picks the winning probe per panel per Decision #20.
7. **ConvLSTM v3 (z-score target, image aug, lr 1e-4, dropout 0.2, batch 32)** gives time-split R² = +0.551, much improved from v1's +0.479. End-to-end supervised baseline now genuinely competitive with frozen-encoder + linear probe (within ~0.12 R² of Geo-SimCLR Ridge time).

## Limitations to remember

- **NDVI quantization:** CropNet B04+B08 are uint8-quantized to ~~5 reflectance levels per channel. Recovered NDVI has ~25-bin precision; affects all encoders' NDVI baselines equally so the comparison stays fair. Continuous-precision NDVI re-download (~~1 day) is a Stage 2 task if needed.
- **DINOv2 band mismatch:** RGB-trained, fed `(R<-Blue, G<-NIR, B<-SWIR1)`. Standard EO-paper practice but not strictly fair. Documented.
- **Prithvi band padding:** expects 6-band HLS (B/G/R/NarrowNIR/SWIR1/SWIR2); we have 3-band AG. Zero-pad missing channels at inference. Documented.
- **CropNet tiles are 9x9 km at 40 m/pixel** (NOT 10 m/pixel as the original proposal assumed). Coarser than ideal but covers more area per tile and aligns with WRF-HRRR weather grid.
- **Cloud filter applied at <=20% per tile** (CropNet); residual pixel-level cloud is normal.
- **USDA yield labels missing for 12% of county-year cells** (small low-acreage counties suppressed for privacy). Affects yield evaluation, NOT SSL pretraining.
- **Stage 1 covers 3 states (IA, IL, IN), 6 years (2017-2022), Apr-Sep, corn only.** Soybean, more states, weather, condition all Stage 2.
